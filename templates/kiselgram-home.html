What is Kiselgram?

Kiselgram is a real-time messaging platform that puts your privacy and experience first. Whether you're chatting one-on-one, running a group for your community, broadcasting to a channel, or hopping on a video call — it all just works.

Built with modern web technologies, Kiselgram delivers a native-feeling experience right in your browser. No downloads, no bloat. Just pure communication.

The best part? The core is completely free. Premium unlocks extra flair — fonts, wallpapers, larger uploads, and more.
